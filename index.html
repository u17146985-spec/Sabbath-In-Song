Welcome

More Than Music.
A Ministry of Worship.

Welcome to Sabbath in Song 2027, a gathering created to celebrate the gift of music and its power to bring people together in worship.

We believe music is more than a performance. It is a ministry, a testimony, and an opportunity to share the love of God through songs of faith and praise.

Join us as we prepare for a special Sabbath experience dedicated to worship, fellowship and a greater purpose.

Where Music Becomes Ministry

Every voice has a purpose. Every song can carry a message of hope. Every note can become an offering of praise. Together, let us use the gift of music to glorify God.

The 2027 Gathering

The Sabbath in Song Imbizo

Sabbath in Song 2027 is an invitation to come together in a spirit of worship, unity and shared purpose. Through music, fellowship and faith, we seek to create a space where people can reconnect with God and one another.
